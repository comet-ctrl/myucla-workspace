# Product direction

A customizable workspace that makes UCLA course planning easier to navigate,
while preserving native functionality. Maintained by comet-ctrl.

## Next priorities

1. **Dependable panel movement.** Clear split/group previews, predictable drop
   targets, keyboard alternatives, and restoration of saved layouts.
2. **Readable course details.** Keep meeting times, rooms, instructors and
   section status together. Improve note tools and avoid redundant controls.
3. **Consistent settings.** Bring layout presets and restoration into one
   understandable entry point, with light/dark parity and visible focus states.
4. **Verified distribution.** Finish authenticated-page checks, capture
   fictional examples from the release build, and prepare a Chrome Web Store
   listing when the installation and privacy materials are ready.

## Design principles

- Keep browsing and the schedule accessible together at desktop widths.
- Let long content scroll within its panel; preserve native controls and focus.
- Show rooms, instructors and status during section selection.
- Make clickable controls discoverable without covering the page in buttons.
- Store layout preferences locally and offer a predictable reset.

DARS interpretation is a separate future proposal requiring its own scope and
privacy review. Automated enrollment, seat polling and account-data collection
are outside this project's scope.

This is a priority list, not a promise of dates or already-implemented features.
